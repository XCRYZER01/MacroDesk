Put MacroDesk on your board

This page writes the firmware straight from your browser over USB. You do not need the Arduino IDE, VS Code or any driver beyond the one Windows installs for the board's USB-to-serial chip.

1 Connect the board

Plug a USB cable into the port labelled USB TO UART, not the one marked USB. The UART port is the one that flashes; the other is the keyboard the PC sees once MacroDesk is running.

If the board never appears, hold BOOT, tap RESET, then release BOOT and try again.

2 Choose the firmware

3 Flash

Flashing replaces the whole application. Your profiles live in the FAT runtime bundle after you use Send to device in Studio. Erasing the whole flash removes that bundle; flashing without erase normally keeps it.

Unplug the cable and plug it into the port marked USB. The PC picks the board up as a keyboard and the deck starts sending shortcuts. A healthy boot logs USB HID keyboard ready (EXIO5 LOW) and Macro Deck UI ready on the serial monitor.
